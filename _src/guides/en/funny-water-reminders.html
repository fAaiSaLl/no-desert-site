Plain reminders get ignored. "Time to drink water" is easy to swipe away; a line that makes you laugh is harder to ignore. Here are real reminders from No Desert's personas. Use them as phone alarms or sticky notes, or let the app send them for you.

The Strict Coach

  • Lifting 250 ml of water shouldn't require a crane, a blueprint and three days' notice.
  • That water bottle has seen more travel in your bag than actual action.
  • If that tumbler stays full any longer, I'm charging it rent for desk space.
  • The replay confirms your hand has hovered near that cup zero times today.
  • A rookie shooting blindfolded hits more targets than your water log today.
  • The match isn't over. Two glasses and we're back in it.
  • You missed the halftime target by a mile. Even the bench is closer.

The Roaster

  • 3,000 unread emails, zero sips. Your inbox wins again.
  • You typed "on my way" from bed. Is the water glass too far too?
  • Forty-seven open tabs, and not one is titled "drink water"?
  • Your phone is at 1%. Somehow, water is still the bigger emergency.
  • That endless scroll has a water-shaped interruption. You're welcome.
  • Your water goal has more unanswered messages than a forgotten inbox.
  • Your water tally is losing the race to absolutely everybody.

Breaking News

  • BREAKING: Local resident continues to pretend the kitchen sink doesn't exist.
  • Sources confirm you spent more time choosing a playlist than lifting a cup.
  • Traffic update: clear roads from your hand to the glass.
  • Weather check: the dry spell over your desk continues. Glass advised.
  • Developing story: the glass is ready. Sources say you're "almost ready".
  • We go live to your cup, now officially part of the furniture.
  • A live report finds your water goal doing all the heavy arithmetic.

Kind Mode (no sarcasm)

  • No need to rush. Your goal is still there, and so is your glass.
  • When you have a moment, pour yourself some water.
  • Morning. The kitchen tap is ready when you are.
  • Water is free, quick and right there. Whenever you like.
  • A sip now, then carry on with your day.
  • You're still on your way. Your next glass is the next step.
  • Give yourself a gentle nudge forward with a glass of water.

Why funny reminders work better

Most people who "don't drink enough water" simply forget. A reminder you actually read beats one you dismiss. No Desert sends a fresh line every day, so it doesn't turn into background noise, and you can log a glass straight from the notification with one tap. If sarcasm isn't your thing, Kind Mode keeps it gentle.
